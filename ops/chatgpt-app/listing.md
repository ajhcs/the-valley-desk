## Long description (limit 4,000)

The Valley Desk is a finder for healthcare research connected to the nine-county Delaware Valley: Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery and Philadelphia in Pennsylvania, and Burlington, Camden, Gloucester and Mercer in New Jersey.

Ask ChatGPT to search the directory's research software projects and publications by subject, institution, topic, county or date. Each result links to its Valley Desk page and shows how the work is connected to the region.

What you can ask

- Find research software or publications on a subject, such as sepsis prediction or medical imaging.
- Narrow by institution, topic, county, publication date or the date a record joined the directory.
- See what has been added since a date.
- Open one record for its regional connection, the source for that connection, and linked code or papers.
- List the institutions, topics and counties the directory covers, with listing counts.

What the labels mean

Verified means a source ties the work to the region. Listed means an observed association, such as an account or indexer match. A listing does not establish employment, ownership, a worksite or clinical validation. Dates are labelled as published (when a paper appeared), pushed (when code last changed) or added (when the record entered the directory).

Good to know

The app is read-only and needs no account. It searches research records and does not search by author. It gives no medical advice. Records removed from the directory are not returned.
