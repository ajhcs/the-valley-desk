# WP03 private enrollment and corpus handoff worksheet

Blank template only. Complete one controlled copy outside Git; do not place
names, source bodies, case-specific records, credentials, or completed copies in
the repository. A filled worksheet is coordination evidence, not authentication,
permission, enrollment or a freeze. Use independently verified records and
references described in the [intake guide](../wp03-intake.md).

For each private assignment record: case ID and exact context digest; reviewer
principal and authenticated authority reference; conflict and independence
disclosure and steward disposition; rubric revision; source IDs and authorized
material scope; private delivery channel and time; restrictions and blinding
undertaking; return receipt and time; incident/exposure and resolution. Keep
delivery and first returns separate until both are complete.

## Per-source rights and evidence

For each source version record source ID, provider/native identity, revision,
observation time, representation digest, provenance/origin family, evidence
receipt and authorized storage location. For each of **fetch, retain, annotate,
evaluate**, record the actual authority/permission receipt, issuer, material
scope, terms and expiry or review date; record unknown or denied uses and the
resulting hold. Confirm the intended reviewer can inspect the representation.
Do not infer permission from public access or a receipt reference alone.

## Selection, independence and exposure handoff

- Record the development set and held-out frame, source scopes, time window,
  frozen selection method, selection records, exclusions and reasons. Plan 360
  canonical held-out cases, 60 per primary route, with eligible, ineligible and
  ambiguous cases per route; a second route appearance earns no extra quota.
- Record an independently assembled eligible recall frame of at least 100 cases,
  source scopes, time window, assembly method and receipts; it is assembled
  without product retrieval, published output or model suggestions as truth.
- Record the admitted-audit frame definition, interval and selection procedure;
  WP24 executes at least 100 independent admitted items. Challenge material is
  separate from prevalence and audit denominators.
- Record reviewed person, project, origin and equivalence families and the
  evidence for merges and non-merges, including mirrors, versions and transitive
  links. Keep development and held-out families disjoint; retain clustering and
  the complete held-out-connected history keys. Distinct works do not merge
  merely because they share a creator or project.
- Record current and historical exposure, access, tuning and rubric revisions,
  with authenticated history and receipts. Unknown history is not proof of
  freshness. Exposed development material and connected families do not become
  fresh holdout by changing IDs. Record geographic and organization-type gaps;
  do not fabricate cases or assume Philadelphia coverage represents the region.
- Record exact source/case context, independent first returns, disagreement
  comparison, adjudication and all authenticated references. Record each
  revision, replacement, failed attempt and re-attestation, preserving originals.
  Review the complete handoff independently before any accountable freeze.
