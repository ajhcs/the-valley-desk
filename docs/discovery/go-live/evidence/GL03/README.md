## Bounded result

frontiers contain seven repository candidates and one publication candidate.
They have no public publication authority. The same-name account repository and
the biological-networks publication have no established health-tech/regional
eligibility. Every remaining candidate also needs GL04 review.

The original five page IDs are settled and cannot receive another permit. The
supplemental plan has only two different IDs, at most one request per provider,
and a two-candidate capacity. Consequently, within these two immutable reviewed
plans, requests are at most `5 + 2 = 7`, GitHub at most `4 + 1 = 5`, and Crossref
at most `1 + 1 = 2`. The six retained original leads plus at most two new leads
give at most eight; their exploration allocations are Philadelphia six, Camden
two and Mercer zero. These allocations are budget labels, not geography claims.
All are below GL02's request, provider, queue, lead and county ceilings.

crash. The original request allowance is 125 seconds and the supplemental
allowance is 50 seconds. The second plan reduces the original total and county
active-time ceilings by the original charged allowances. Manual exploration

The final [activity accounting](activity-budget.json) conservatively charges the entire first-issue-to-last-response wall window to each explored county, as well as all request allowances: 3,700,156 ms total, with every county below 1,800,000 ms. This includes interleaved method engineering; post-capture verification is separate. These
are conditional bounds under the trusted operator, locked runtime, honest

## Replay and reproduction

`replay-used.mjs` is the exact operator replay harness; the two preparation
scripts are preserved for audit. Their imports are relative to their original
location, `.discovery-work/gl03/root/` in the checkout. To rerun on this operator
host, copy the replay harness there and run it with the qualified Node runtime
and a rehydrated private batch directory. Preserve the original paths in each
frozen binding. The harness opens a **new** private replay directory, reissues
journal permits without HTTP, imports the retained packets, checks exact replay
idempotence, closes/reopens and compares candidate identities and origins.
Do not rerun either capture batch or preparation script as a replay procedure.

`nativeBodySha256` commits discarded source bytes. `projectionSha256` commits the
retained facts array; `packetSha256` commits the complete retained packet. Native
JSON pointers describe the inspected field mapping. Retained-packet replay
cannot reconstruct the native response or independently prove the projection.
Source facts remain method-qualified operator attestations requiring GL04
corroboration and field review. No region-wide recall, independent benchmark,
human annotation, public admission or deployment credit follows.

`validation-summary.json` distinguishes the full regression run from the final
focused rerun after supplemental refinements. Its counts overlap.
