The [manifest](GL02-exploration-manifest.json) fixes the initial aggregate and per-route bounds before GL03. For each new method, complete source-specific policy, exact-request and retention preflight first; this may authorize the first strictly bounded qualification probe within the current user scope. A failure leaves the affected route pending without blocking unrelated qualified routes.

## Methods and field-use decisions

These are separate from the existing adapter DTOs and synthetic tests. Each planned new route needs the [independent source review](../../qualification/independent-source-review.md): installation/endpoint and exact request, terms and access/robots where applicable, field/material scope, permitted retention/transformation/redistribution, parser and pagination, actual safe receipt, limits and a reviewed disposition. A preflight is distinct from the later actual receipt: it establishes the safe bounds for the first probe, and the later review establishes whether that method can proceed beyond it. Credentials and contact configuration are references to operator-managed configuration, never values in these files. No paid service, private account, standing crawler, source activation, or new persistent service is implied.

## Decision and evidence handling

1. Before a qualification probe, bind exact scope, provider/installation, rules, dates, parser and public factual fields to the frozen batch and confirm applicable authority. Then inspect the actual safe receipt and parser result before broader collection or enrollment. If response material cannot be safely limited, stop rather than retain it. Use only public resources; exclude contacts, credentials, abstracts, full HTML, source bodies and README text.
2. Capture derived facts and safe URLs with source, date, exact route and direct/indexed distinction. For an actual request retain minimal safe receipt, status, count, checkpoint and any refusal; never promote indexed or synthetic output to a native request receipt. Per-source terms govern retention and publication. The existing qualification's historical retained bodies are not authorization to collect new bodies.
3. Account enumeration exhausts source pagination within the manifest cap without star, recency, paper, or prestige selection. Page caps or blocked responses make the account incomplete. Repositories and authors are separate identities; only independently supported dated relationships can connect them to a campus or project. Verify facts, canonical/fork/archived state, relationship direction and project-specific health relevance before admission. No publication is required; paperless is a qualification route, not a claim that no paper exists.
4. The frozen [diagnostic](GL02-known-project-diagnostic.json) records known examples and unknowns before tuning. Compare actual outcomes as found, missed, ineligible, unknown, refused or pending; Sol independently checks eligible misses. Old examples are development-exposed, and do not count as novel coverage or an independent benchmark. County and route denominators count the frozen planned exploration, never the unknown regional population.
5. GL04 retains authority over review and public projection. GL09 reports actual county/route attempts, incompleteness, account enumeration, representation, unknowns and next actions; GL10/GL11 determine readiness. Nothing in GL02 authorizes publication, deployment, outreach or recurring operation.

## Frozen request envelope

The manifest fixes a maximum 25-second outer deadline and 48,000 decoded bytes per response, identity encoding only, no redirects or retries, and at most five records on one page for the first qualification probe. Inspect the full response and every field against the approved metadata and rights scope before any retention; an unexpected field, non-identity encoding, oversize response, redirect, timeout or malformed response stops the probe and remains incomplete. Apply stricter source-specific or transport bounds where required. These are proposed batch ceilings, not provider maxima or a claim that every existing transport implements them. The 48,000-byte ceiling is no greater than `maxResponseBytes` in `src/ingest/discovery/runtime-contract.ts`; the other controls require verification at the actual request boundary. Expansion requires a revised independently reviewed manifest.
