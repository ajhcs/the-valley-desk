# Frozen sample review

The [pre-label protocol](GL08-yield-protocol.json) preserves the exact 100 native
IDs selected from the 549-repository seed cohort. No rejected, held, poorly
described, forked or archived case leaves the denominator. A useful purpose,
supported health-tech relevance, a source-backed dated regional connection and
an original link are required. Unknown required evidence stays held. Optional
papers, biographies and current-person verification are not added prerequisites.

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| [Eligible fraction](GL08EligibleFraction.json) | Supported eligible cases / all 100 reviewed leads. |
| [Minutes per admission-ready listing](GL08MinutesPerAdmissionReady.json) | Total timed agent review minutes across eligible, rejected and held leads / eligible cases with a prepared lean listing. Undefined when eligible count is zero. |

Review sessions record actual monotonic timestamps and the host boot ID.
Evidence-read latency and decision recording are included; setup, independent
code review, fixes, tests and CI waits are excluded. Per-lead duration stays
unmeasured when only a batch interval is observed. Initial metadata display
occurred during setup before timed decisions and is excluded. These are agent
elapsed times, not a human operating-capacity estimate. Actual public catalog
admission is separate and remains unmeasured.

The cohort reflects selected seed accounts and accepted source pages, including
incomplete account inventories and account clustering. Its descriptive result
cannot establish regional yield, recall or clinical validity. Original native
responses were transient; source receipts and short source-specific review
notes identify the evidence and its limits.

The reviewed sample yielded **7 eligible leads out of 100 (7%)**. Another
26 were rejected and 67 were held. The review clock recorded **27.92 agent
minutes**, or **3.99 agent minutes per admission-ready listing**. The numerator
includes screening, evidence reads and recording all decisions, including
changes after adjudication. Four amendments timed only the recording step.
Independent challenge time is excluded. Public admissions were zero, so
minutes per public admission are unmeasured.

The initial review found seven eligible, 31 rejected and 62 held. The independent
review moved five rejects to held. One documented a clinical demo with an
unverified regional basis. The other four lacked enough evidence to rule out
health-tech relevance. The reviewer also strengthened two decisions without
changing their dispositions. A dated regional basis was unresolved for 65 of
the 67 held leads. Unknown criteria can overlap.

The [results receipt](GL08-yield-results.json) pins the sample, both sets of
decisions, timing journal and independent review. The full evidence and private
listing drafts remain in the workspace.
