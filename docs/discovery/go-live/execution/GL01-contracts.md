# GL01 directory v1 contract

`src/content/discovery-directory.ts` defines a new strict, bounded directory-work and snapshot shape, independent of the existing pilot v3 and the original discovery store schemas. It is not wired to a Worker, source fetch, D1, UI or publication action. This is an implementation and review artifact, not a production admission or source-authority receipt.

## Minimum and optional fields

A v1 work carries a stable internal ID and positive revision, one of `repository`, `publication`, `preprint`, `discussion` or `project`, source-bound title and safe HTTPS link, a checked date, source-observation references, and reviewed health-purpose and dated regional-connection claims. Both claim kinds are required structurally. The evidence objects contain a URL, observation date, observation hash, pointer and observation method; the hash is a reference, not a claim that this schema fetched or verified bytes. `reviewRefs` bind claim/relation review IDs to receipt hashes within the work. Names and hashes are structurally checked; GL04 must reconstruct observations, assess permitted use, validate exact subject/field/value/time bindings and verify real reviewer authority before a reviewed projection can be published.

Native identity is either a source-bound provider/native-ID pair or null; a standalone project does not need a provider ID. Optional native description, owner and source date are separately evidence-referenced. Owner is a source-scoped User, Organization or other label, with optional native ID; it is not a resolved person or institution. An absent publication, person, affiliation history, biography, photograph, external license or popularity field does not invalidate a useful listing. A source date is separate from `checkedAt`; every referenced observation date must be no later than the checked date; neither is a present employment or currentness claim.

A regional claim records optional institution, campus and municipality, explicit PA/NJ state, one of nine counties or an unknown county, a county/municipality/campus basis, date precision, explicit subject role and label, source-author index when the subject is a publication author, scope qualifier, and affiliation and geographic evidence roles. The selected basis requires the corresponding county, municipality, or campus plus institution; state and county must agree. A county may remain null when the cited geographic evidence supports a municipality or campus connection. The affiliation and geographic evidence roles each require a reference, including when the county is unknown; the actual nine-county connection remains a GL04 evidence determination. Both roles can point to the same observation if it actually supports both; no second-source or paper-affiliation requirement is implied. `subjectId` binds the claim to the directory work, while the source-scoped subject label/index explains whose affiliation is meant; it does not assert that a person, their worksite, their account and the repository are identical. Scope labels do not themselves establish current employment. Existing regional contexts are separately sourced interpretations and must never fill native author-affiliation arrays during compatibility work.

Evidence IDs, review-reference IDs, claim IDs and relation IDs are unique in their respective work; every claim's evidence and review IDs, every source-field evidence ID and every relation's evidence/review IDs must resolve within that work. A region's role-specific evidence must also be included among its claim evidence. A snapshot rejects duplicate work IDs, canonical native identities, global claim IDs, conflicting definitions for a reused evidence or review ID, unresolved relation targets, repeated relation IDs, duplicate directed relation triples and wrong endpoint kinds. Identical shared observations may be reused. GitHub and PubMed numeric IDs are canonical; Crossref DOI values normalize case and a doi.org URL prefix; arXiv aliases normalize the URL prefix while explicit versions remain distinct. Other providers require an HTTPS installation namespace and retain opaque local IDs exactly, so identical local IDs from different forums do not collide and unproven Unicode equivalence is not invented. Cross-provider identity or version equivalence is never inferred. Typed directed relations distinguish repository-cites-publication/preprint from publication/preprint-links-repository. These relations do not imply person identity, code authorship or the reverse direction. A broader identity graph, generic arbitrary relations and current-role inference are outside this increment.

## Topic vocabulary and admission cases

`DIRECTORY_TOPIC_REVISION` fixes the initial `directory-topics-v1` vocabulary in the module: medical imaging, sleep, machine learning, clinical informatics, biomedical data, assistive technology and public health. The explicit `ai` alias points to machine learning; it is a query aid, not a classifier. A topic claim requires its own evidence and review reference. Actual health relevance and any AI label require source-based review; the schema cannot detect a fabricated or misleading claim from valid strings alone. Topic expansion is a versioned future change.

Positive structural examples in the focused test cover a paperless User-owned repository, a standalone project with null provider identity, independent publication/preprint/discussion works, incomplete optional people and a region supported by one shared observation. Negative cases cover missing health purpose, missing source/ref, mismatched subject/author index, invalid or undated region, invalid dates or hashes, unsafe URL, private extra field, unknown topic, wrong relation direction and missing relation target. A local AI project or star count without a real healthcare contribution is not admissible: the missing-purpose shape fails, while a dishonest but syntactically valid health claim requires GL04 evidence review. A matched name cannot establish an account-person link; there is no automatic person-merging function.

## Pilot compatibility and limits

The 28 existing v3 record objects remain untouched; this increment does not convert or replace the runtime catalog. Pilot source identity/title/URL/description/date and repository owner can be mapped only with their actual source pointers, and the nine directed citation observations can inform matching v1 relation predicates. Historical regional contexts can inform separately reviewed region claims with their own subject, date and affiliation and geographic evidence roles. Missing fields and source-author associations stay absent or unresolved; pilot organization-background records are not silently converted into works. Existing v3 author arrays and every source field remain preserved in the original artifact; v1 does not yet provide a full author list or claim to reproduce it. GL04 must define the actual reviewed adapter, evidence reconstruction, authorization, correct migration and full old-record preservation checks before any public switch. Snapshot syntax does not validate a source hash against a file or confer authority, source rights, public enrollment or clinical truth.

## Explicit downstream extensions

GL01's v1 is a work-level compatibility contract, not a complete discovery or identity graph. Professional/project role links, independently identified person or account pages, source-reported archive/fork/successor state, first-seen history and correction/removal references are not represented as public v1 fields. Do not infer them from omitted fields or attach them as unvalidated extra JSON. GL01 retains source date and checked date as distinct values; first-seen remains unknown. GL04 must define reviewed versioned additions, private state, source binding and removal behavior before any of those features are emitted. GL07 can expose professional links only when their typed identity and evidence contracts exist. New fields require a versioned schema and compatibility review; absence here does not prohibit a lean paperless listing.


## Private frontier and URL fragments

`DirectoryPrivateFrontierSchema` in `src/ingest/discovery/directory-frontier.ts` is a separate strict staging envelope for pending, needs-evidence, duplicate, ineligible and ready-for-reviewed-projection outcomes. A collection-lead entry embeds the existing `DiscoveryCollectionLead` unchanged, preserving its literal `publicationAuthorized: false`, and records provider, source, scope and page-receipt identifiers, request/representation hashes and lead index. A manual lead records only an HTTPS source link, date and observation digest. The public DTO module does not import the private ingestion module; the private wrapper reuses the public primitives and the existing collection lead. The outer envelope also requires `publicationAuthorized: false`; a ready disposition is a proposal, not authority or an automatic public write. Needs-evidence, ineligible and duplicate dispositions require a reason, and duplicate requires a distinct target ID; GL03/GL04 must resolve references to the actual draft/page, source scope, state and real review authority before any projection. The schema does not fetch a URL. Private candidates, associated notes and contact material stay outside public serialization.

Safe HTTPS public and evidence links may include fragments, supporting discussion posts and line citations; installation namespace identities prohibit queries and fragments. This distinction does not grant permission to retain or reproduce linked content.
