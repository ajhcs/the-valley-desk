The tests exercise all six acceptance criteria:

- AC01: a qualified Crossref-only work query commits original bytes, private publication candidates and page receipts through actual D1 without a GitHub or PubMed prerequisite. Repeated native DOI with changed metadata retains distinct source versions and a contradiction review hold.
- AC02: case, `doi:` and canonical HTTPS variants within the documented bounded ASCII subset resolve to one native identity. Unsafe or lossy URL, percent-encoding, traversal and unsupported non-ASCII forms are rejected; this is not a claim that every valid DOI is supported. The DOI Foundation documents [ASCII case folding and URL encoding](https://www.doi.org/the-identifier/resources/factsheets/doi-resolution-documentation/).
- AC03–04: identical name-only authors remain unresolved; no catalog person or regional admission is created. Year-only online and month-only print dates remain distinct from newer deposited/indexed metadata. Retained replay reproduces private metadata after the live claim expires.
- AC05: actual provisional-source evidence is refused by the independent-origin counter. Separately reviewed synthetic Crossref and PubMed representations initially count as two origins; after an explicit reviewed origin revision places both members in one original, they count as one. DOI equality alone never performs this adjudication.
- AC06: oversized response, unqualified cursor, unresolved relation endpoint, item and page caps stay incomplete. A genuine three-page chain exercises the store's ancestry lookup; bad mapping and corrupted first-page payload fail closed. Continuation query-hash substitution, missing/corrupt parents, invalid clock/claim timestamps and expired claim are rejected. Historical replay remains usable after continuation expires. [Crossref documents cursor pagination and its five-minute expiry](https://api.crossref.org/swagger-ui/index.html).

Metadata is private provisional output; license hints are not metadata collection permission or an admitted reuse claim. Page receipts do not create SourcePage, catalog subjects, independently reviewed provenance, accepted geographic claims or public authorization.
