## Findings requiring owner disposition

1. **Current exposure family leakage.** The `history.exposures` check compares only exposed IDs against current heldout IDs. A differently identified recall or challenge case can share person, project, origin, native identity or representation keys with an exposed heldout family without triggering this check. Prior-revision family history does compare keys; the same protection is needed for current exposures, including unresolved exposure IDs. Protocol says fresh cases and families must be untouched. Preserve the original finding and add a synthetic counterexample.
2. **Critical-identity gate on nonpublic candidates.** Reproduced `summarizeDiscoveryEvaluation` with one captured eligible item, `admitted:false`, no critical errors: it returned `criticalIdentityGate:true` while admission precision was unavailable and overall acceptance remained `not_evaluated`. E01's gate concerns audited public output; absence of errors among candidate-only rows cannot satisfy it. Make public audit scope explicit or keep that gate unavailable/false, and test the counterexample.
3. **Mixed cohort denominators.** The metric observation has no audit/reference membership field, while one helper computes both admission precision and recall from all supplied rows. If audit and independent recall rows are mixed, admitted reference-only rows enter precision and audit-only rows enter recall; even if callers filter, one invocation cannot express overlapping populations explicitly. Encode the selection scope or require separate typed populations, with a mixed/overlap test. This preserves EX07's denominator distinction rather than forbidding overlap.
4. **Input receipt freshness.** The preparation receipt records an earlier shared-interface hash; IF02 changes occurred after its creation. Recompute the receipt after the contract and implementation are final and retain prior hashes and failure history.
