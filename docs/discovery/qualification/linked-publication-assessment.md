## Access, rights and format boundaries

Public bibliographic records and source pages were consulted for this bounded assessment. [Crossref's metadata policy](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) distinguishes reusable factual bibliographic metadata and CC0 Crossref-generated data from abstracts, whose copyright remains with authors or publishers. A bibliographic metadata-only retention and redisplay proposal may be reviewed under that policy; it does not grant rights to the abstract, full paper, illustrations, source-code README, contact information or linked websites. PMC availability is not an article-specific CC BY license. The 2018 paper displays SPIE copyright; the 2020 PMC copy is an author manuscript; no CC BY grant for either was verified. QSIPrep's article-specific license likewise remains unverified. Abstract and full-text retention and redisplay are on hold pending explicit version-specific rights; neither full article nor contact-bearing native source is staged in the repository. Software licensing is separate from publication rights.
