Status: entry-method review, not corpus acceptance. Real source qualification activity does not itself populate or freeze WP03. The actual reference manifest remains empty and `not_frozen`; no actual human labels, reviewer enrollment, signing keys or accountable freeze were created.

## Evidence provenance is not a reference label

A real request and retained representation digest establish what bytes were observed at a particular endpoint and time. A separately reviewed permission basis establishes the allowed uses. Neither establishes that a forum account is a person, a professional is currently employed locally, an institution's headquarters is the author's actual unit, a technical assertion is true, or two mirrored records are independent origins.

The readiness evaluator separately expects authenticated evidence and permission statements. The permission payload binds the source descriptor and the uses `fetch`, `retain`, `annotate`, and `evaluate`. A policy URL, robots rule, capture hash, agent review or local fixture capability cannot replace those signed assertions under real operator-provisioned trust. Existing CLI success never issues a freeze and must continue reporting `not_frozen`, `freezeIssued: false`, and `packageAccepted: false`.

## Staging boundary for this work

Keep candidate capture references in a qualification/preparation ledger until accountable entry conditions are met. Preserve exact source/provider/installation identity, endpoint and source revision, observation time, original representation hash and storage/removal reference, rights-policy evidence and permitted uses, parser outcome/version, proposed family/origin links, unknown fields and exposure history. Avoid invented receipt references or placeholder signatures that resemble authenticated evidence.

Do not write model-generated labels into independent-human annotation slots. Proposed geography, relevance, identity and family groupings remain proposals. Require actual independently authorized reviewers, the accepted rubric, blinding, preserved disagreement and accountable adjudication. Permissions must cover the intended originals and annotation/evaluation use; dataset licensing does not transfer rights in linked pages.

Any real sample inspected by implementation agents to repair parser behavior or rules is development-exposed. Record that exposure and its case/family relationships. Do not silently recycle such samples as untouched held-out evaluation material. Sampling frames and route balance, independent eligible recall assembly, family leakage checks and the prespecified later admitted-item audit procedure remain separate requirements. A conveniently reachable sample is not a representative denominator or recall roster.

## Exact context and re-attestation

The current selection-v2 expectation binds manifest policy/rubric/design/revision, full case descriptors, source identity/version/origin/time/representation snapshots, and partition sampling. Annotation-v2, blinding-v2 and adjudication-v2 bind the same context digest; adjudication also binds the exact annotation set and receipt references. Replacing a real source, changing case meaning, or correcting a county/family/rubric requires deliberate re-review and re-attestation. Old labels cannot follow a new source context automatically. No legacy fallback or signature migration is permitted.

The signed store authenticates under separately pinned operator policy; it does not decide whether the assertions are true. Synthetic keys and permissive fixture authorities remain test-only even if paired with genuine captured bytes. Current revocation/expiry policy, actual authority grants and accountable freeze review remain external prerequisites.
