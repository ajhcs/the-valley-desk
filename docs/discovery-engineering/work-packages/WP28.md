# WP28: Conditional legacy retirement and consumer transition

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Sol  |  **Phase:** AFTER  |  **Dependencies:** WP27  |  **Status:** deferred

## Purpose and contract

Deferred and not a core-release blocker. Under R15–R17, C_LEGACY and P09, P20 and P28, investigate legacy retirement only after exact-candidate public operational acceptance and an explicit new operational scope. Existing news, events, Watch routes, APIs, feeds and consumers remain supported until a controlled transition is separately approved. A product navigation change does not authorize deletion, source retirement, disabling a scheduler or redirecting an unrelated identity.

Inventory endpoints, consumers, data ownership, subscriptions, jobs, retention obligations, redirects and recovery dependencies. Identify each consumer and owner where possible; distinguish unknown consumers from none. Compare a proposed replacement's identity and semantics against each legacy path, including feed and calendar behavior. For an identity-preserving replacement, prepare a versioned transition, communication and compatibility window, rollback steps, verification checks and a responsible owner. Any communication requires its own explicit authorization. Preserve historical records and evidence according to the approved retention policy.

Prepare the concrete scope, expected impact and stop conditions for the operational decision. Rehearse using isolated fixtures; monitor actual consumers only within authorized scope. If identity, owners, rollback or critical consumer impacts remain unresolved, retain the legacy service and escalate rather than silently removing it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP28-01:** Given the legacy route inventory, each known consumer and unknown ownership has a disposition and retained evidence.

2. **AC-WP28-02:** Given a proposed redirect, identity and semantic equivalence are demonstrated or the original remains supported; retain comparisons.

3. **AC-WP28-03:** Given a concrete retirement proposal, an approved scope, transition window and tested recovery precede action; retain rehearsal and decision.

4. **AC-WP28-04:** Given an unresolved critical consumer or failed rollback, stop and retain legacy support; document owner and next review.

## Future validation, handoff and recovery

For a future approved transition, retain the consumer inventory, direct-response comparisons, rehearsal logs, rollback manifest and explicit owner decisions. Test identity-preserving redirects and feed compatibility in isolated fixtures before any cutover. If a critical consumer, retention dependency or recovery path cannot be verified, stop and keep the existing route or service available; do not treat an unknown consumer as absent.
