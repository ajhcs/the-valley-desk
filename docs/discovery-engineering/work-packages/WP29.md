# WP29: Deferred expansion and funding experiments

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Astra  |  **Phase:** AFTER  |  **Dependencies:** WP25, WP27  |  **Status:** deferred

## Purpose and contract

Deferred and not a core-release blocker. Explore future source expansion or optional funded monitoring only after WP25 operating evidence and WP27 operational acceptance, under R06–R07 and R16–R18, F_DISCOVER and F_COVERAGE, P05–P06 and P22–P25, and E03–E04. The basic public catalog remains free. No customer, budget, demand or funding is asserted by this package and no outreach is authorized now.

For a proposed expansion, describe the concrete audience need, provider and installation, permitted source scope, expected benefit, data and rights, access method, qualified identifiers, resource and review capacity, measurable outcomes and failure behavior. Enroll and qualify an additional channel independently under the existing source contract; a new API, patent source or project website is not automatically eligible. Preserve known gaps and avoid extrapolating an unknown regional denominator. Review changes against geography, relevance and public/private boundaries.

For a funding hypothesis, ground the decision in observed demand and operating capacity, define the optional monitoring service without restricting the basic catalog, and specify an experiment with a real owner, measurable stop criteria and caps approved before execution. Any funding approach, outreach, external commitment, paid service, collection or operational change needs a concrete future scope and appropriate authorization. Record negative or inconclusive evidence and decide whether to defer, revise or stop.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP29-01:** Given a proposed source, its actual permissions, independent discovery path and failure cases are qualified before enrollment; retain E04 evidence.

2. **AC-WP29-02:** Given an expansion proposal, capacity, source coverage and measurable success criteria are grounded in observations; retain scope and owner.

3. **AC-WP29-03:** Given a funding experiment, actual demand evidence, caps and a separately approved scope precede outreach or commitments; retain decision record.

4. **AC-WP29-04:** Given inconclusive demand or inadequate capacity, record deferral and preserve free catalog access; retain follow-up criteria.

## Future validation, handoff and recovery

Retain the future source qualification receipt, documented demand evidence, experiment charter, spending and hours caps, observations and decision to continue, revise or stop. Validate permitted source access and failure replays before any enabled collection. If demand or capacity is unsubstantiated, defer the proposal; if permission or qualification fails, leave the source disabled and record the gap.
