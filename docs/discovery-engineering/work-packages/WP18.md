# WP18: Home, work, people, organization and project journeys

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Sol  |  **Phase:** I06  |  **Dependencies:** WP17  |  **Status:** not_started

## Purpose and contract

Build reader journeys for R03–R05, R10–R14 and R18 using VIEW_PORTAL, F_BROWSE and F_DISPLAY; P06, P12–P15 and P19–P21 constrain results and E05–E06 test the experience. Provide Explore, Work, People and Organizations as primary navigation, while retaining legacy news, events and Watch access under WP19. Make the public catalog free.

Create search and browsing states for work and people with exact API filters, sort, date precision and normalized query reflected in the URL. Respect OR within a facet and AND across facets. Offer clear code, publication/preprint, project and discussion facets without making any one source a prerequisite. Distinguish an empty result for the selected query from unavailable, failed, unsearched or limited source coverage. Allow recovery from loading, failure and retry states without misrepresenting gaps as zero results.

Provide work detail, person and organization views, and project views where an admitted relationship exists. Work detail must stand alone without a repository or project; a person can have a professional profile with no indexed work. Show purpose, creator role, date and precision, current or historical regional basis, healthcare-technical relevance, publication stage and last-supported activity when admitted. Explain unknowns instead of filling them. Source evidence, rights or unknown reuse terms and a supported next action such as the original source, documentation, contribution channel or published professional contact should be accessible without internal workflow identifiers.

Provide a public `/methodology` route and component explaining enrolled source scopes, coverage windows, known gaps, the meaning of no matches, and how corrections are submitted and reviewed. Consume the allowlisted public coverage DTO owned by WP21; keep operator-only details private. WP18 can use a frozen-contract fixture until WP21 delivers the DTO; WP23 verifies the integrated result. Coordinate direct routing and crawler parity with WP19.

Use only WP17 public projections for discovery records. Continue pages on immutable snapshot versions; on a material invalidation or expired cursor, show the 410 state, preserve the user's query and offer an explicit restart. Never silently drop changed records. Initially expect primary reads and no-store delivery. Browser navigation, direct links and refresh must agree with Worker behavior in WP19.
