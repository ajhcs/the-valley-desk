# WP15: Review workspace and suggestion or correction intake

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Astra  |  **Phase:** I04  |  **Dependencies:** WP14, WP16  |  **Status:** not_started

## Purpose and contract

Implement a review experience for accountable admission and public suggestions or corrections under R04, R08, R12 and R14. F_REVIEW and F_LINK with P04, P07, P12 and P14 require supported claims and authenticated decisions; E01, E02 and E06 test accuracy and correspondence. Suggestions and model output are leads, never publication authority.

Show claim, source and literal evidence span, provenance origin, observation date, policy version, existing decision, conflicts and unknowns. Present distinct person, account, organization, work and project identities; prevent name-only joins and unsupported grouping. A reviewer can accept, reject or defer each admissible claim with a reason, identity and expected evidence/policy head through the WP14 API. Detect stale decision inputs and require reload and reconsideration rather than overwriting another decision. Surface permission failure and service unavailability without suggesting a decision was saved.

Provide a public suggestion and correction form that requests only information needed to review the issue, identifies the affected public record and accepts supporting public sources. Validate and bound input; display submission receipt or a clear failure. Do not expose internal candidates, private notes, reviewer identity beyond approved public fields, secrets or unreviewed submissions. The authenticated workspace is separate from public views. Send intake into the same evidence and review process rather than writing public projections. Correction, withdrawal or rejected suggestions need a visible operator disposition and retained history, with retention and abuse protections specified by the security and operations work.

Use keyboard-accessible forms, status announcements, source links and clear distinctions between conflicting claims, missing evidence and verified facts. Coordinate with WP21 for queue status and follow-up. Integrate approved-correction status with WP16 guarded publication and invalidation; a review decision alone does not establish public completion.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP15-01:** Given a suggestion with a source, intake yields a reviewable receipt and no public change; retain API and browser evidence.

2. **AC-WP15-02:** Given ambiguous identical names and contradictory source spans, the workspace preserves distinct identities and presents evidence without an inferred merge; retain reviewer trace.

3. **AC-WP15-03:** Given an outdated head or unauthorized actor, decision submission fails visibly without overwriting accepted state; retain concurrent and authorization tests.

4. **AC-WP15-04:** Given an accepted correction, authenticated review records authority and reason and delegates publication to guarded WP16; retain decision history and public/private boundary checks.

## Future validation, handoff and recovery

Using isolated fixtures, exercise suggestion submission, decision retry, stale heads and a reviewed correction through IF04, IF07 and IF08, then run the focused review UI and API tests. Retain browser observations, request/response transcripts with sensitive fields removed, candidate revision and decision history. If WP16 publication or invalidation fails, preserve the review decision and show the correction as pending; reconcile its operation key and heads before retrying instead of claiming publication.
