## Slice contract and steps

Choose development cases with independent institutional creator evidence, an independently discovered PubMed paper and an independently discovered GitHub repository. Include an additional creator with no indexed work and a paper without code to prevent accidental repository requirements. Retain source/provider identity, provenance origin, dates, evidence spans and labels; synthetic integration fixtures must be labeled synthetic and cannot establish empirical truth.

Capture through the real adapter/store interface, resolve typed identities and preserve ambiguous author/account links as unresolved. Demonstrate a creator-current regional claim separately from a work's creation-time regional attribution in the exact nine-county DVRPC scope. A current local role cannot retroactively localize earlier nonlocal work. Only supported relationship evidence may connect the selected repository, paper and project; the slice must still work when that relationship is withheld.

Submit through the protected review flow. Record authenticated decision authority, expected revisions, evidence and policy heads. Publish immutable allowlisted creator/work rows with complete dependency-vector atomic guards; a model suggestion cannot approve the candidate. Display creator, paper and repository through real Worker/API/UI paths with evidence links, rights distinctions, source-date precision and useful original-source actions.

Query work and people independently, follow detail links and paginate a valid snapshot. Inject a supported correction or affiliation expiry between pages. Verify old continuation returns 410 with restart and preserved filters, corrected public rows become coherent, and historical evidence remains inspectable according to policy. Repeat capture to verify stable identities and idempotency, and simulate one typed provider failure without fabricating a healthy empty result.

Review the end-to-end traces for contract mismatches and assign bounded corrections to owning packages. Do not expand the slice into whole-goal acceptance: Crossref, arXiv and forum independence, full held-out evaluation, broad accessibility/performance evidence and operating sustainability remain downstream gates.

6. **AC-WP30-06:** Given replay and provider failure, identities remain stable and health is typed without erased evidence; evidence is before/after state and failure receipt.
