# WP20: Accessibility, performance and interaction qualification

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Astra  |  **Phase:** I06  |  **Dependencies:** WP15, WP18, WP19  |  **Status:** not_started

## Purpose and contract

Qualify the R12–R13 reader and review experiences under VIEW_PORTAL, F_BROWSE and F_DISPLAY, with P13–P15 and P20–P21 and empirical E05–E06. Target WCAG 2.2 AA through both automated and manual evaluation of actual rendered states; an automated pass alone is not conformance. Include public discovery, details, sparse records, source evidence, forms, errors, correction intake and authenticated review where accessible to the test role.

Exercise keyboard-only traversal, logical focus order and restoration after filter, pagination, dialog, error and navigation; confirm visible focus, skip navigation, labels, accessible names, announced status and errors, meaningful headings, contrast, target size, zoom, reflow, touch and reduced motion. Test representative screen-reader/browser combinations and document versions, operating systems and limitations. Test narrow and wide layouts and high zoom. Include empty search versus coverage gap, loading, unavailable source, unknown rights, sparse person and work without a project, 410 restart and preserved query. Provide alternatives for information conveyed solely by color, icons or motion.

Define and freeze representative device, network, dataset, routes and sample sizes before measuring load and interaction performance. Record Core Web Vitals targets and observed distributions, including LCP, INP and CLS where measurable, plus payload and error behavior; targets are test criteria, not proof of universal performance. Use supported local synthetic state and exact candidate build, with cold and warm runs clearly identified. Avoid promising speed based on a single Lighthouse score. Retain failures and regressions, prioritize fixes and repeat affected checks.

Use formative development checks to examine whether intended readers can find work, understand scope and rights, and reach an appropriate next action. WP24 separately owns the independent final E05 evaluation and disposition. Accessibility and reader usefulness are separate measurements. Hand unresolved blockers to the orchestrator before release qualification.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP20-01:** Given representative routes and states, the frozen WCAG 2.2 AA criteria are met through automated and manual keyboard, screen-reader and zoom checks; retain versions, results and defect retests. Reporting failures alone does not pass.

2. **AC-WP20-02:** Given empty, failed, sparse and expired-cursor states, readers receive distinct understandable feedback and can operate recovery by keyboard; retain formative observations; final E05 disposition belongs to WP24.

3. **AC-WP20-03:** Given frozen performance targets and test conditions, measurements meet those targets for representative pages under the frozen conditions; retain distributions, traces and exact candidate ID. Reporting a miss does not pass.

4. **AC-WP20-04:** Given a material accessibility or reader misunderstanding, fix and pass the affected recheck before package acceptance; otherwise retain the unresolved release-blocking disposition and failed evidence.

## Future validation, handoff and recovery

Run the repository’s focused accessibility and browser checks against the exact future candidate, plus manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom and reflow sessions. Record tool and browser versions, routes, individual failures and retest traces; retain performance measurement conditions and distributions alongside formative task observations; WP24 retains final E05 results. A material defect blocks qualification until fixed and the affected checks are repeated, or explicitly escalated as an unresolved release blocker.
