## Protocol and gate decisions

Freeze proposed thresholds with accountable product/editorial acceptance before exposure to results. Evaluate the sealed 360-case held-out corpus, 60 cases per primary route, separately from the production-like admitted-item audit and independently assembled recall roster. Preserve group separation by people/projects/provenance and annotate cross-route duplicates; no system-generated output becomes its own truth set.

E01 audits creator identity, person/account distinctions, regional claim type, supported health-tech role and dates. Require zero observed critical person/account mistakes or false-current claims in the audited sample. An old local affiliation or local adopter cannot establish present regional creation. Report errors and exposure denominator; zero observed errors is not proof of zero population risk.

E02 audits at least 100 admitted independent items. Require relevance precision at least 95% and the prespecified two-sided 95% confidence-interval lower bound at least 90%. E03 measures recall against at least 100 independently assembled eligible cases: at least 80% recall and lower bound at least 70%. Fix adjudication, ambiguity and missing-data rules beforehand; unresolved positive predictions count conservatively against precision. Report route and exact nine-county geography breakdowns, dates, selection probabilities where known, confidence method, clustering and nonsampling limits. Enrolled-scope recall never implies coverage of an unknown regional population.

E04 retains actual permission/access qualification, bounded transport, provider/native identity, provenance, paging/checkpoints and typed failure recovery for GitHub, PubMed, Crossref, arXiv, forum and institutional routes independently. Missing or inaccessible routes block whole-goal acceptance; a repository-backed paper does not demonstrate independent publication acquisition.

E05 uses at least 12 independently recruited representative readers, preassigned tasks and success rules. Require at least 10 of 12 complete their assigned task sets and no repeated major misconception, such as confusing historic affiliation with current employment or access with reuse permission. Include representative correction/expiry cadence from WP17: retain restart counts, usable snapshot lifetimes and their effect on task completion. Freeze the disposition rules before seeing results; recurrent restarts that prevent assigned tasks or repeatedly confuse readers require repair. Report per-task outcomes, accessibility, unsuccessful actions and recruitment limitations.

E06 incorporates WP23 exact-candidate correspondence/CI and relevant WP22 qualification. E06 preproduction qualification can pass here; separate public operational confirmation remains pending WP27 and is not a WP24 prerequisite. A material candidate change requires affected evaluations to rerun under the versioned protocol.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP24-01:** Given frozen labels and audited identities, E01 reports every critical error and blocks on any observed case; evidence includes blinded adjudication and denominators.

2. **AC-WP24-02:** Given admitted-item audit outcomes, E02 meets both precision gates on at least 100 independent items; evidence includes reproducible counts and intervals.

3. **AC-WP24-03:** Given the independent eligible roster, E03 meets both recall gates and exposes source/geography gaps; evidence includes misses, exclusions and sampling limits.

4. **AC-WP24-04:** Given each route, E04 documents prior qualification and actual bounded success/failure behavior; evidence contains redacted provider receipts, not fixtures alone.

5. **AC-WP24-05:** Given 12 readers' assigned tasks under representative correction/expiry cadence, E05 meets completion and misunderstanding gates, including the preregistered restart-usability disposition; evidence contains task-level observations, restart/lifetime measurements and reviewer decisions.

6. **AC-WP24-06:** Given the candidate evidence bundle, E06 distinguishes preproduction checks from pending release acceptance; evidence is the signed gate matrix with no inherited or circular PASS.
