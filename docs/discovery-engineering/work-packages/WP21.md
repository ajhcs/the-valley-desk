# WP21: Refresh, coverage, corrections and operator controls

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Sol  |  **Phase:** I07  |  **Dependencies:** WP07, WP14, WP15, WP16, WP17  |  **Status:** not_started

## Purpose and contract

Terra owns this planned operational backend and its operator interface, with Sol reviewing. Implement R07, R09, R11, R14 and R16 through C_COLLECTOR, F_REFRESH and F_COVERAGE, P10–P11, P20, P25–P26 and P28–P30, evaluated under E03, E04 and E06. Source checks and correction foundations begin in WP05–WP07; this package integrates them into operating controls rather than inventing them for the first time.

Show per-source enrollment, scope, last attempt and last successful observation, typed health, checkpoint, retries, limits and known coverage gaps. Record the denominator and observation window for counts. An empty enrollment, failed, unavailable, truncated, permission-limited or unsearched scope cannot establish complete or empty coverage. Preserve old evidence after failed refresh; do not infer current affiliation or activity from an old observation. Provide authorized, bounded pause, retry and review actions with actor, reason and audit history. Enforce budgets and durable fair scheduling from WP07 and preserve checkpoints on interruption.

Accept corrections through WP15 and trace them to source evidence, decision history, affected claims and projections. A material evidence, policy or accepted-claim change must atomically protect dependencies and advance the invalidation epoch through WP16. Public immutable projection versions support WP17 snapshots; expired continuation returns 410 with preserved query, not silently dropped records. Initially use primary reads and no-store. Record source status, attempted correction, reviewer disposition, withdrawal and recovery without exposing candidates or operational metadata publicly.

Own an allowlisted public coverage DTO for the WP18 `/methodology` page, with enrolled source scopes, observation windows, known gaps and correction-process information. Derive it from scoped coverage receipts, distinguish unavailable and incomplete scopes from empty results, and exclude private candidates, credentials and operator-only details. Publish under the WP16/WP17 public delivery contract; WP23 verifies the integration.

Define monitoring and alert ownership, runbook thresholds, incident hold and escalation paths. Keep a queue of overdue reviews and freshness exceptions; do not fabricate healthy status or automatic publication. Coordinate all real collection, scheduler changes, migrations and service activation under a separately scoped operational action.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP21-01:** Given failed, truncated and empty-enrollment scopes, operator views preserve evidence and report distinct gaps with denominator and window; retain typed health tests.

2. **AC-WP21-02:** Given interrupted collection and a bounded retry, checkpoints persist and counters and scheduling remain within WP07 contracts; retain synthetic replay.

3. **AC-WP21-03:** Given an accepted correction, guarded publication advances invalidation and an old cursor yields 410 while query is retained; retain concurrency and audit evidence.

4. **AC-WP21-04:** Given an unauthorized pause or review action, state stays unchanged and the attempt is auditable without leaking private data; retain authorization tests.

5. **AC-WP21-05:** Given complete, failed, unsearched and truncated scopes, the public coverage DTO reports the correct source scope, window and gaps and describes correction review without private fields; retain fixture, allowlist and response tests for WP23 integration.

## Future validation, handoff and recovery

Replay interrupted collection, source failure, stale heads and correction races in isolated synthetic state; retain checkpoint and budget state, sanitized operator audit records, epoch changes, HTTP outcomes and candidate revision. Run focused operations and concurrent-publication tests after implementation. If a refresh fails, preserve prior permitted evidence, mark the source unhealthy and resume from its committed checkpoint under the authorized retry policy; never refresh a claim by failure.
