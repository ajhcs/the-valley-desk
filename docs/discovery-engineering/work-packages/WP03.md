## Corpus and measurement contract

Recruit adjudicators with access to original public evidence, retain disagreements, and record rubric version, label, rationale, source/version, observation time and confidence. Use positive, negative and genuinely ambiguous examples. Include ambiguous names, accounts mistaken for people, expired affiliation, local adoption without creation, generic AI, independent papers, creators without work, partial dates, retractions, mirrors and unsupported project joins. Current regional presence and regional creation at work time require separate labels within the exact nine-county DVRPC region.

Freeze a development set and a disjoint 360-case held-out set with 60 cases assigned to each primary acquisition route: GitHub, PubMed, Crossref, arXiv, forum and institution. Assign underlying people, projects and provenance-origin families to one partition before selecting cases. DOI versions, preprints, repository mirrors, syndicated institutional pages and account aliases must not cross partitions. Cross-route duplicates retain one canonical case and one primary route for counting; retain other route appearances as attributes. If these rules prevent required quotas, expand independent collection or hold the freeze.

Build an eligible recall roster independently of the system's retrieved or published output, with at least 100 independently assembled eligible cases and declared source scopes/time windows. Do not construct evaluation truth from model suggestions, accepted system decisions or successful search results. Select and document the separate audit sample of at least 100 admitted independent items without cherry-picking. Define person/account and false-current error severity before inspection.

Freeze proposed pilot gates for WP24: relevance precision at least 95%, two-sided 95% interval lower bound at least 90%, and zero observed critical identity or false-current errors; recall at least 80% with lower bound at least 70%; at least 10 of 12 readers complete assigned tasks with no repeated major misconception. Use a prespecified binomial interval method, such as Wilson, only where independent-item assumptions fit; disclose clustering and nonsampling bias. Freeze route/geography breakdowns, denominator exclusions and handling of ambiguity before execution. Ambiguous cases remain visible and cannot inflate true positives; report a conservative analysis treating unresolved positive predictions as errors. These are proposed thresholds requiring policy acceptance, not achieved results.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP03-01:** Given the case manifest, a partition audit finds exactly 360 held-out cases, 60 per primary route, and no overlapping identity/project/origin families; evidence is the versioned manifest and overlap report.

2. **AC-WP03-02:** Given conflicting or incomplete sources, adjudicators preserve ambiguity and independent rationales; evidence is a blinded disagreement and adjudication log.

3. **AC-WP03-03:** Given the recall roster, provenance review establishes assembly independently of system outputs and at least 100 eligible cases; evidence records scope, dates and selection method.

4. **AC-WP03-04:** Given a metric calculation, every numerator, denominator, interval method and exclusion is reproducible; evidence includes worked positive, ambiguous and clustered examples.

5. **AC-WP03-05:** Given later tuning or rubric changes, the protocol blocks reuse of exposed held-out cases for a fresh claim; evidence is a version/change procedure requiring a newly frozen evaluation set.
