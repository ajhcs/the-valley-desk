# WP01: Product, geography, relevance and business policy

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Astra  |  **Phase:** I01  |  **Dependencies:** WP00  |  **Status:** not_started

## Purpose and contract

Define the free public WHO/WHAT/WHY/FIND/SORT/DISPLAY catalog for regional healthcare technology, informatics and AI. R01–R03, R06, R10 and R18 implement F_QUALIFY, F_RELEVANCE and F_COVERAGE; P01–P03, P05–P06 and P31 govern admission, with E01–E03 measuring reality. This policy is input to WP02, not a declaration that existing data already satisfies it.

The exact geography is Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery and Philadelphia counties in Pennsylvania; Burlington, Camden, Gloucester and Mercer counties in New Jersey. A person's current professional regional presence differs from historical affiliation and from residence. Work qualifies through evidence of a regional creator role when it was created; current local adoption alone does not establish local creation. Explicitly test a creator moving into the region after work was created and moving out after creating work locally. Unknown dates or county remain unknown.

Require both healthcare purpose and a technical development, application, evaluation or explanation. Incidental computer use, routine clinical content, generic AI, and unrelated local software do not suffice. GitHub, PubMed, Crossref, arXiv, qualified public forums and institutional sources are independent entry paths; people may qualify without indexed artifacts and papers or discussions without a repository. Separate creators, accounts, institutions, projects and artifacts. Leave ambiguous identities and relevance for review.

The accountable editorial owner must decide how to allocate limited capacity to discovery, including reducing new general-news or policy production when discovery needs require it. Record the priorities, capacity assumptions and decision authority. Existing news, events, Watch routes and consumers remain supported independently of that editorial allocation; this decision does not authorize a service shutdown.

The basic catalog is free to readers. Optional funded monitoring may be explored only after demonstrated demand and a separate scoped decision; do not invent customers, budget or outreach. Freeze policy version, adjudication authority, exceptions and test labels before tuning; retain disagreements for review.
