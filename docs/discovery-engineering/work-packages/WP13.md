## Implementation and interface contract

1. Enroll explicit public institution, lab, association and independent professional pages, declared feeds and bounded directories. Discover only declared feed links and qualified navigation; do not guess feed paths or crawl whole sites. New hosts/endpoints require source qualification, not an automatic follow from an observed link.

2. Create creator-first candidates with professional health-tech basis, dated institution/unit affiliation and supported county evidence. A person with no indexed artifact may qualify. Keep institution headquarters, actual professional unit location, residence and service area separate; a member of a multi-site institution is not automatically in a local county.

3. Extract supported creator roles, publisher roles, works and declared relationships as typed evidence. Association publications and independent creators stay eligible. An organization publishing work need not be its author; a local hospital adopting external software does not establish regional creation.

4. Use ORCID/ROR identifiers or other public professional identifiers only as supported leads under separately qualified access. Matching identifiers/links can propose equivalence; names alone cannot merge people. Retain historic affiliations, unknown current status and contradictions for review rather than promoting old biographies to current facts.

5. Preserve date precision, source versions, rights and exact excerpt/structured evidence. Check directory pagination/roster completeness explicitly; excluded or inaccessible members remain coverage gaps. Use WP06 outputs and budgets consistently, including manual leads through the same observation/admission boundary. Estimate recurrent profile refresh and identity-review labor.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-WP13-01:** Discover an independent professional with no repository/paper; a creator candidate and supported public-profile inputs exist; retain graph fixture.

- **AC-WP13-02:** Observe an association-published relevant output; publisher and creator roles remain distinct and eligibility does not require university employment; retain relationship evidence.

- **AC-WP13-03:** Use headquarters in Philadelphia but an author unit outside the nine counties; current-local admission is withheld; retain positive/negative location cases.

- **AC-WP13-04:** Use an old profile or unknown affiliation expiry; preserve historical evidence without asserting current employment; retain temporal boundary trace.

- **AC-WP13-05:** Encounter a declared off-scope feed, guessed feed path or private contact data; no unauthorized collection/public field results; retain request/projection audit.

- **AC-WP13-06:** Process a partially paged directory and duplicate mirrored biography; report incomplete coverage and one provenance origin; retain denominator and origin ledger.
