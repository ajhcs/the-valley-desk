## Implementation and interface contract

1. Implement an independent bounded query route with enrolled categories/terms, time window, page size and total-result cap. Treat query hits as review candidates; category membership or an AI keyword does not establish health-tech relevance. Support a preprint with no repository, journal DOI or project grouping.

2. Parse only qualified Atom namespaces/fields and stable arXiv identifiers. Store the continuing preprint identity separately from source version IDs and retained metadata revisions. A later source version is related explicitly to its predecessor; a journal DOI becomes a proposed typed relation requiring WP14 admission.

3. Preserve original submission and later update dates with their source fields, stage=preprint and correction/withdrawal status when supported. Do not label a journal reference as proof of peer review or treat a fresh fetch as new scientific activity. Reuse claims require admitted license evidence; access and source collection rights remain separate.

4. Retain author names, explicit affiliations and public identity links as evidence-backed leads. Sparse affiliations and ambiguous names remain unresolved; institution evidence may support local-at-creation while current-local requires its own dated basis. Do not invent missing affiliation or infer residence from collaboration.

5. Bind every page to normalized query/window, enrollment/configuration, parser version and request continuation. Enforce WP06 request/byte budgets and durable acknowledgments. Detect missing entries, duplicate IDs, changed totals and truncation; describe the searched scope and partial result state rather than promising complete coverage.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-WP11-01:** Discover a preprint from an arXiv-only scope with no other artifacts; it enters review with its own identity and stage; retain route receipt.

- **AC-WP11-02:** Observe v1, v2 and repeated v2 metadata; one preprint identity, distinct source versions and idempotent replay result; retain graph readback.

- **AC-WP11-03:** Present a journal DOI and same author spelling; only a proposed relation is created and no automatic person merge or reviewed-stage upgrade occurs; retain claims.

- **AC-WP11-04:** Provide no affiliation or county evidence; regional admission stays unresolved while the preprint candidate survives; retain review hold.

- **AC-WP11-05:** Change ingestion time without source timestamps and supply unknown license; date order/reuse claim does not improve; retain normalized projections.

- **AC-WP11-06:** Return foreign-namespace entries, truncated pages or rate limits; reject/quarantine safely and report incomplete coverage; retain negative receipts and counters.
