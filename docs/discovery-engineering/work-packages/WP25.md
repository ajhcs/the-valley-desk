# WP25: Operating pilot, capacity and business sustainability

**Owner:** Terra  |  **Reviewer:** Astra  |  **Phase:** I08  |  **Dependencies:** WP21, WP24  |  **Status:** not_started

## Purpose and contract

Evaluate whether the free basic catalog can be operated sustainably under R14, R16–R18, F_COVERAGE and F_INTERPRET, P25–P26 and P30, using E03–E05 and E06 results. This is a measured isolated or preproduction enrolled trial before public launch, with an explicit operational scope and no dependency on WP27, not a statement that a product, customer base or funding already exists. WP24's whole-goal empirical acceptance and WP21 controls are entry dependencies, not substitutes for actual operating observations.

Before any pilot activity, name an accountable operating owner and backup, define the pilot cohort, enabled qualified source scopes, baseline backlog, expected review and correction service levels, incident escalation, privacy and retention arrangements, and stopping criteria. The default is a four-week observation window; extend it when incidents or too few operating cycles make the inference inadequate, subject to approved caps and scope. Preregister measurable backlog size and age limits, review and correction service levels, capacity and queue-stability criteria, and staff-hour and cost criteria for a release-ready decision. Fix explicit spending and staff-hour caps before the pilot; record the approver and source of the caps without inventing numerical budgets in this plan. Execute only within separately authorized scope and use the correct environment. No source, scheduler or deployment activity is authorized by this document.

Measure arrivals, completions, backlog age and size, correction handling time, source failure and recovery, time and costs, and coverage denominators and gaps over the agreed observation window. Assess queue stability from arrival versus completion rates and backlog trends, accounting for the finite window and unusual incidents. Record operator workload and the ability of the backup to take over; retained evidence must distinguish measured results from assumptions. Report unmet service levels, capacity bottlenecks and uncertainty, and stop before a cap is exceeded.

The basic public catalog remains free. Optional funded monitoring is a later hypothesis only after real demand, operating capacity and a separately scoped decision are evidenced. Do not invent customers, budgets or outreach. Separate completion of the study and its evidence from a `release_ready` decision. The accountable operating authority may mark `release_ready` only when the preregistered backlog, service, staffing, cost and queue-stability criteria are met within approved caps, owner and backup coverage is established, and material uncertainty is resolved. A completed study that recommends stop or records a failed or inconclusive criterion cannot satisfy WP27; retain a hold and remediation/retest plan. Produce a decision record for continuation, adjustment or stop, signed by the responsible operational authority; a model proof or a successful engineering test cannot establish editorial capacity or business sustainability.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-WP25-01:** Given a proposed pilot, named owner and backup, caps, duration, permitted source scope and stop conditions are approved before activity; retain charter.

2. **AC-WP25-02:** Given pilot observations, report arrivals, completions, backlog age, hours and costs against fixed caps and service levels; retain dated measurements.

3. **AC-WP25-03:** Given rising queue or cap exhaustion, pause within the agreed scope and document recovery or closure; retain incident and decision records.

4. **AC-WP25-04:** Given a completed pilot, distinguish study completion from `release_ready`: verify preregistered backlog, service, staffing, cost and stability criteria and the owner/backup coverage against measurements; retain the signed decision. Failed or inconclusive criteria hold WP27 pending remediation and retest.

5. **AC-WP25-05:** Given a negative stop result or insufficient operating cycles, record a hold and a concrete remediation or extended observation plan; study completion alone cannot authorize WP27.

## Future validation, handoff and recovery

Retain the approved trial charter, time-stamped queue and source-health samples, operator hours, spend, incident log and owner/backup observations. Compute arrivals and completions for the same window and compare them with backlog age and size; report uncertainty and any omitted intervals. If a cap approaches, incident interrupts normal operation or cycles are insufficient, pause or extend under the charter and record the decision rather than extrapolating stability.
