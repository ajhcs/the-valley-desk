# Accepted direction, November 13, 2026

The goal is to help people find useful regional healthcare work and connect with its creators.
The system must identify who is in the area, determine what they work on, establish relevance, and support finding, sorting and displaying the information.
The selected geography is the nine-county DVRPC region in Pennsylvania and New Jersey.
Health technology, health informatics and health AI define the focused domain.
GitHub, publications, arXiv, forums and other qualified sources are independent discovery routes.
A repository is not a prerequisite for admitting a publication, preprint, project or relevant discussion.
The engineer must repair model gaps, prove the mathematically tractable rules, and then produce an implementation plan.
Patents remain deferred from the prior direction. Existing creator identities, source provenance, qualified publication authority and compatibility obligations remain in scope.
