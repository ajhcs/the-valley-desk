# Proposed engineering interpretation

Professional regional presence and affiliation are distinct from residence. Public professional evidence determines the appropriate label.
The regional policy accepts documented creation, authorship, maintenance or publication connected to a selected county. Local adoption alone is insufficient.
Unresolved contributor-only cases remain in review. Historical affiliation does not establish current employment.
Health relevance requires a documented healthcare application and a technological, informatics or health-AI contribution.
Keyword and name matches create candidates. Accepted evidence and qualified decisions govern public claims.
Projects are continuing efforts. Repositories, publications, preprints and discussions are artifacts that may relate to those projects.
A work item can remain independent when no project grouping or linked code is established.
Person, organization, account, project and artifact identities remain distinct. Proposed cross-source matches do not become silent merges.
The proposed query combines OR within a facet and AND across facets. Sorting uses explicit match, source-event date and stable identity keys.
Pagination uses an immutable result snapshot and a query-bound cursor. Source observation time never becomes a fabricated publication date.
Public cards show supported purpose, creators, regional basis, source/version dates, publication stage, reuse terms and unresolved fields.
Coverage reports describe enrolled source scopes and retained cohorts. They do not claim complete regional or Internet coverage.
Provider admission requires a qualified access route and bounded collection policy. API availability alone does not establish source access, complete metadata or publication rights.
Proofs concern explicit rules and assumptions. Source truth, relevance judgments, recall and usefulness require independent empirical evidence.
