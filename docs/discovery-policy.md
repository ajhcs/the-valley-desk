## Geography and time

Only these exact state-qualified county keys are in scope: `PA:Bucks`, `PA:Chester`, `PA:Delaware`, `PA:Montgomery`, `PA:Philadelphia`, `NJ:Burlington`, `NJ:Camden`, `NJ:Gloucester`, `NJ:Mercer`. County name alone does not resolve a state. `NJ:Delaware`, `PA:Mercer`, any other explicit county and any unknown or unverified county do not positively qualify. Explicit outside evidence is outside; missing or conflicting evidence remains unknown or disputed, not outside by assumption. The fixture includes every key, same-name different-state examples and unknowns. A legacy `core` flag, city, mailing address, institutional headquarters, membership, or local adoption is not by itself a county-qualified professional relationship.

For a person, the default people result requires evidenced **current professional** presence in an eligible county and a substantive health-technology professional basis. A dated, supported historic association can be found in historical mode with its dates and status; an unknown end is not proof of current presence. Residence alone is neither professional presence nor a reason to disclose a home location. An independent creator can qualify through reviewed public professional evidence without an employer, repository or indexed work. No person is admitted solely because their name matches an account, author or other person.

For work, require an evidenced substantive creator contribution performed in an eligible county at its relevant creation or contribution time. A local contributor can qualify the supported contribution to a wider project; mere membership, stars, distribution or current local adoption do not. Where original development is evidenced elsewhere and no substantive local creator contribution is supported, regional eligibility is false on the supplied evidence. A continuing candidate may be held for review of the unsupported local creator role; absence of evidence does not prove no such contribution exists, and new evidence can reopen the decision. An individual who moved into the region after making work elsewhere may have a current regional profile; that older work is contextual history, excluded from regional-work results and counts. An individual who moved away after making work locally may have a historical profile and qualifying regional work, without a current regional profile. An organization-authored artifact can qualify without resolving optional individual authors. These are separate decisions, not inherited from one another.

Preserve event, observation, review and expiry times separately. Represent a year, month, day or instant at its stated precision as its supported half-open interval; do not fabricate a day, a move date, or a current role from an observation date. A temporal overlap that could cross a relocation or county boundary is unresolved unless other evidence establishes the relevant contribution location. With an active positive date filter the entire supported interval must fit the requested interval; an unknown date cannot match. Without a date filter an otherwise eligible unknown-date record is not excluded solely for that reason, but unknown contribution timing cannot establish a required local-at-creation link. Conflicting claims stay visible to review, with their provenance and decision history; do not infer precision or resolve by majority of duplicated copies.

## Relevance, identity, rights and decisions

A positive relevance decision requires both a healthcare purpose and substantive technology, informatics or health-AI development, application, evaluation, or explanation. Clinical material with only routine computer use, generic AI with no healthcare purpose, local software unrelated to healthcare, and local adoption alone are insufficient. Missing or conflicting purpose or technical contribution is review, not a negative fact. A qualified healthcare technical explanation may be a paper, preprint, repository, professional profile, institutional work or public forum discussion. GitHub, PubMed, Crossref, arXiv, qualified public forums, and institutional/association sources are independent entry routes; a repository, project or indexed artifact is never a universal prerequisite.

Keep creator, source account, organization, project, artifact/version and their relationships distinct. A credible source-scoped account is not automatically a named person; shared names, co-occurrence and institutional affiliation do not resolve an identity or authorship. A forum work can qualify without a person join only when its required substantive local creator role is independently supported; the account and any person remain separate identities. Preserve source, claim, time and any conflict for each relationship. A work may be qualified without an optional individual join, while a required creator or geographic relationship that is unsupported remains in review.

Assess source qualification and authority to fetch, retain, transform and display separately. Evaluate rights for each artifact version and material scope; public access is not reuse permission. A relevant and regional item with unknown or restricted display rights remains relevant and regional, but its affected material is held until its permitted display is established. A qualified link or independently permitted metadata may be displayed only if supported by their own authority and dependencies. Model suggestions and synthetic labels are not publication authority. Final public publication requires valid evidence, identity, geography, relevance, applicable rights, dependencies and an authenticated accountable review or qualified deterministic rule. No exception bypasses those requirements: new or exceptional policy cases are held for accountable adjudication, recorded with rationale, evidence, date, policy revision and any disagreement. Changes to policy require a new version and dependent contract/test review; never change labels merely to manufacture agreement.
